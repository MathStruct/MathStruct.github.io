MathStruct develops open-source tooling for mathematics, scientific computing and AI/ML, and
most of what we write lives in the projects' vaults: the theory, the design decisions and the
implementation notes of [[projects/lenticulum-jl|Lenticulum.jl]], [[projects/sophia|Sophia]]
and [[projects/moonkale|Moonkale]], next to the code they describe.

This blog is for what does not belong in a vault:

- **releases**: what a new version can do that the last one could not;
- **results**: a measurement, a negative result, an idea that did not work and why;
- **essays**: how the projects fit together, and where they are going.

Posts are signed and dated, so it is always clear who is speaking. Every post has a comment
thread at the bottom: sign in with GitHub and say what you think.
