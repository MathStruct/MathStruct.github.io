> Every blog post lists its authors from the `authors:` field in its
> frontmatter, and each name links to the matching heading on this page. So the
> heading below must be spelled exactly like the name in the frontmatter —
> `## Daniel Boigk` ↔ `authors: [Daniel Boigk]`.
