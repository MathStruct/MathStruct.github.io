> [!info] Adding yourself
> Add an entry to `landing/src/lib/people.ts`: the people page and your own page, in English
> and Chinese, are built from it. Then create `content/people/your-name.md` (the file name is
> your name in lower case, with hyphens for spaces) for the site's search, and add a row to the
> table above. Blog posts with `authors: [Your Name]` then link to your page.
