Short, undated pages: definitions, worked examples, reading notes, things that
want to be linked to rather than read front to back. Blog posts argue; notes
just state.


Start a note by copying `content/templates/Note.md`, or read the
[[guides/authoring|authoring guide]] first.

## Suggested shape

A note is one idea: a definition, the statement of a theorem, a construction.
If it needs sections and a narrative arc, it is probably a
[[blog/index|blog post]].
